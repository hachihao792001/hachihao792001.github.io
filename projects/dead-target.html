DEAD TARGET: Zombie Games 3D by VNG GAME STUDIOS (opens in a new tab)

Contributed Unity, C# 2022 – 2023

The first game that I worked on in Madpoly studio - VNG Corporation. It is a big opportunity for me to work in such a big game, there are many challenges, one of them being understanding the huge and old codebase.

One of the most intricate features I've implemented is the Arch of Thunder, a unique eagle-shaped crossbow capable of firing and CONTROLLING lightning arrows.

It grew into a system of its own: a tiered evolution path for the bow, a per-scene config for when the camera is allowed to fly along with the arrow so it never reveals a part of the map it shouldn't, and several rounds of polish on top.

What else I shipped

  • 16 weapon skills: Plasma Flare, Sweet Embrace, Yokai's Strength, Slasher, Artic Glacier, New Year Resolution, Scorched Earth, Apollo's Descent, Pulse Cannon, Burning Love, Thunderstruck, Pot'o Gold, Atomic Strike, Arcane Detonation, Final Judgement, Graviton Surge.
    I think the most complex skill is Plasma Flare, it shoots a barrage of plasma projectiles that move in a curve path, and spawn timed bombs on the objects that they collide with on its way.
  • Defense skills for drones: At the start of each battle, player's drone will drop traps around player.
  • Better jump animation for zombies: Use Catmull-Rom spline to correct the jumping arc for zombies, and also freeze jump animation when zombie begin falling so zombie only play the land animation when they've actually landed.
