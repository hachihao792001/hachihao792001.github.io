Zombie Shoot Out: Offline Game by VNG GAME STUDIOS (opens in a new tab)

Contributed Unity, C#, PlayFab 2026 – now

The game I'm currently on at Madpoly studio - VNG Corporation, and the one I was assigned to right after conscription.

What I shipped

  • Team system: Manage player team (group) with Playfab. Implement Team-related rank-based LiveOps events: Team Exploration, Team Battle and Team Tournament.
  • Event V2: Rework the entire time framework for every old and new LiveOps events, handling old to new conversion so a new event only starts once the previous one (old version) has finished and no one loses progress mid-run. Built six new events: Rescue Rush, Jeep Racing, Vaccine Formula, Supply Drop, Virus Trace, and Glory Showdown.
  • Facebook: Connecting and disconnecting an account, use Playfab to link Facebook account to in-game data, get friend lists and their in-game data, inviting friends into the game through Messenger, and sharing a win or an achievement on Facebook. I actually have written a blog post about this one, check it out here.
  • Friends system: Friends leaderboard, send and accept/deny friend requests, suggest friends based on mission progress, last online. Also use Playfab.
  • Google Play Achievements on Android.
