  • W, A, S, D to move

  • P to change controlling object
  • I, K, J, L to move controlling object.
  • U, O to move controlling object up and down
  • B, N, M to rotate controlling object around the X, Y, Z world axis
  • When looking at companion cube, E to pick it up
  • When holding companion cube, E again to throw it
  • When holding companion cube, F to let go of it
  • When not holding companion cube, F to reset its position and velocity
  • G to toggle gravity for companion cube
